# The Enchan Field — v1.0.5

**An Effective Field Framework for Geometric Stabilization and Non-Linear Relaxation**

**Release date:** November 19, 2026

Version 1.0.5 is a mathematical clarification of the existing effective-field proposal, not a new physical derivation or a new numerical experiment.

## Changes from v1.0.4

- **Variational consistency (§3.3–3.4):** The normalized response is `μ(𝒳)=(2/a₀²)∂𝒦/∂𝒳`. This restores the coefficient required by the displayed Lagrangian and gives `μ→1` in the specified weak-gradient limit.
- **Finite-tension interpretation (§3–4):** Distinguishes the response coefficient `μ(D)`, transmitted magnitude `|D|μ(D)`, and kinetic function `𝒦`. Reduction of one does not imply boundedness of the others.
- **Scope of stability and convergence:** Clarifies that screening alone does not prove global regularity, bounded evolution, or asymptotic convergence; extra constitutive and dynamical conditions are needed.
- **Cross-section consistency:** Aligns the abstract, introduction, discussion, and conclusion with these limited claims.
- **Graph evidence (§7):** The original large-graph results are identified as observational reports rather than controlled, screening-only matched ablations. The published scores and figures are retained.
- **Version and documentation:** Includes `REVISION_v1.0.5.md`, with details of the source revisions.
